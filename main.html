Dhurandhar follows Jaskirat Singh Rangi (Ranveer Singh), an Indian operative recruited from death row. He assumes a deep-cover alias in Karachi's ruthless Lyari underworld to infiltrate a dangerous nexus of gangsters, politicians, and the ISI. He risks everything to dismantle terror networks targeting India from within.
